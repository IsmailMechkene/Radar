# Source spike (October 2026)

Live check of candidate sources for Radar (SPEC section 5). Every claim below comes from a response
or page fetched on 2026-10-16 with `scripts/source-spike.mjs` (User-Agent
`Radar/0.1 (personal internship search app; local-first; …)`, sequential, at least 1.1 s between
requests, a few requests per source). Trimmed raw samples are in `fixtures/<source>/`.

Field key: **T** title, **C** company, **L** location, **R** remote flag, **D** description,
**P** posted date, **DL** deadline, **U** URL, **V** visa info.

## Notes for the adapters

- **Keyword matching must use word boundaries.** Cohere's "Senior Full-Stack Engineer, Internal Applications" matches a naive `/intern/`.
- **Internship detection should combine fields and titles.** Ashby's `employmentType: "Intern"` misses half of Mistral's internships; Arbeitnow mixes English and German `job_types` (`internship`, `Praktikum`, `Werkstudent`, `working student`).
- **Descriptions are HTML** (Greenhouse double-escapes it as entities). Store plain text; never render HTML.
- **Deadlines** are rare in feeds. Greenhouse has `application_deadline` and WWR has `expires_at` (a listing expiry, not an application deadline). Most deadlines will come from description text (M5 parser).
- **Attribution.** Arbeitnow, Adzuna and RemoteOK ask to be credited or linked. Radar is personal and shows each posting's source and link, which covers that if these are used.
- **Politeness** for the fetch layer: honor robots.txt (Remotive must be refused), `Crawl-delay` (Lever 1 s, RemoteOK 1 s, InstaDeep 3 s), at most 1 request per second per host, and a few calls per day for aggregators.

## Addendum: more boards and platforms (2026-10-19)

Checked live through Radar's own fetch layer (`src/core/http/client.ts`: robots.txt, at most one
request per second per host) and adapters, one request per board.

**Boards on the existing adapters.** 138 candidate boards were tried: 104 answered,
31 do not exist under the guessed name, and 3 are larger than the 10 MB
response limit (Anduril (greenhouse), OpenAI (ashby), SpaceX (greenhouse); left out). 76 were added to `seeds/sources.json` (each
`tos_note` records the job and internship counts seen that day): boards with internships open now
(Waymo 61, Veepee 47, Palantir 41, Doctolib 34, Jump Trading 31, Coinbase 26, Lyft 24, IMC 19,
Stripe 14, Databricks 13, Pinterest 13, …) and AI, developer-tool and European companies that hire
interns by season (Anthropic, ElevenLabs, Synthesia, LangChain, Cognition, Wayve, Pennylane, …).
US-only boards without internship history and very small boards were not added.
